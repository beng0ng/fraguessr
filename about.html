About Fraguessr

A daily guessing game about what perfumes are made of, and where in the world those materials come from.

How to play

  1. Every day there is one famous perfume, the same for everyone. A new one arrives at midnight Pacific time.
  2. Name the notes you think are in it, like bergamot, rose or vanilla. Each right one lights up on the globe where it grows.
  3. You have five drops. A wrong note costs one and sharpens a clue. One hidden note always has a clue, and finding it passes the clue on.
  4. Each note you name joins your collection and deals one of its fun facts. Rarer notes score more.

Between dailies you can explore the Scent Atlas: 354 ingredients and 602 perfumes on a 3D globe, with the story behind each one.

Where it comes from

Perfume note lists, ingredient origins and facts were gathered from public sources such as encyclopedias, perfume houses’ own descriptions and fragrance references. Every fact card links the source it came from.

A perfume’s published notes are a description, not a formula, and can change between editions. The place shown for an ingredient is where it is best known from, not necessarily where a given perfume sourced it.

Fraguessr is an independent fan project. Perfume names, houses and brands belong to their owners, and Fraguessr is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. The bottles and ingredients you see are original 3D renders, not product photos.

Privacy

Fraguessr has no ads, sells nothing and uses no tracking cookies.

  • Your progress stays in your browser. Your streak, collection, facts, stats and settings are saved in this browser’s local storage, and they disappear if you clear this site’s data. They only leave this device if you create an account.
  • Shared statistics, accounts and online rooms use Google Firebase. The site signs your browser in anonymously, without asking for anything, so a finished daily counts once in the shared totals (how many players found each note). An account is optional: it keeps a copy of your progress so it can follow you between devices, along with the email or Google account you sign up with. In an online room, the name and colour you pick and your guesses are shared with the other players while you are in it, and are removed when you leave.
  • Things the page loads from elsewhere. Fonts come from Google Fonts and the 3D and globe libraries from jsDelivr. Like any web request, those services see your IP address and browser.

Start over on this device? This erases your streak, collection and stats from this browser. It can’t be undone.
